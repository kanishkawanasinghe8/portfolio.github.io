Topology Optimization and Fatigue Life Characterization of Additively Manufactured High-Strength Aluminum Components

2025
Oshan, Dr. R. Vance, K. Arisawa, and M. Chen
Journal of Mechanical Design & Materials Engineering, Vol. 48, No. 3, pp. 214–228. DOI: 10.1016/j.mechdes.2025.109842
Abstract: This research investigates density-based SIMP (Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization) topology optimization applied to selectively laser-melted (SLM) Al 7075-T6 alloy components. We model microstructural porosity variations, surface roughness fatigue knockdowns, and anisotropic stress responses under multiaxial cyclic tension-torsion. Experimental S-N curve validations demonstrated an 85% predictive accuracy with an empirical Goodman fatigue model.

Conjugate Heat Transfer Analysis and Hydraulic Performance of 3D-Printed Microchannel Cold Plates for High-Flux Power Electronics

2024
Oshan, S. Mitchell, and T. L. Henderson
International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Vol. 192, Article 122901. DOI: 10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2024.122901
Abstract: As power semiconductor power densities surpass 120 W/cm², conventional finned liquid heat sinks incur unsustainable thermal bottlenecks. Here, we present a bifurcated counter-flow microchannel design manufactured via direct metal laser sintering (DMLS). Three-dimensional Navier-Stokes CFD simulations coupled with conjugate solid conduction were benchmarked against closed-loop test bench calorimeters, displaying heat dissipation of up to 4.8 kW with hydraulic pressure drops < 14 kPa.

Kinematic Synthesis and Disturbance Observer-Based Trajectory Control of a Modular 6-DOF Robotic Manipulator

2024
Oshan and P. K. Sharma
IEEE/ASME International Conference on Advanced Intelligent Mechatronics (AIM), Boston, USA, pp. 412–418.
Abstract: This paper develops a geometric Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) kinematic framework coupled with a non-linear disturbance observer (NDOB) to suppress joint compliance effects in low-cost planetary gear driven manipulator arms. Experimental results exhibit a 42% reduction in end-effector trajectory tracking error during rapid acceleration maneuvers.
