Hello, I'm Oshan

I am a dedicated Mechanical Engineering practitioner with a profound passion for design optimization, kinematics, and thermo-fluid mechanics. My academic journey and hands-on projects have centered on solving multidisciplinary engineering challenges through analytical modeling, rigorous simulation, and experimental testing.

Whether conducting non-linear structural stress simulations in ANSYS, generating complex parametric assemblies in SolidWorks, or programming embedded microcontrollers for robotic end-effectors, I thrive on delivering reliable, cost-effective, and safe mechanical designs.
