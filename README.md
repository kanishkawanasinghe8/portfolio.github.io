# Modern Mechanical Engineering Portfolio

A sleek, modern, multi-page portfolio website designed specifically for a **Mechanical Engineer**, inspired by the dark, high-contrast UI aesthetic of **VOXERA**.

Built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**—zero complex build tools, zero dependencies, and 100% plug-and-play.

---

## 🌟 Visual Design Highlights (VOXERA Inspired)
- **Persistent Top Ribbon**: Frosted-glass backdrop-blur navigation bar with active page indicator, brand monogram, and CTA action buttons.
- **Hero Section**: Glowing monospace status badge (`■ MECHANICAL ENGINEERING & PRODUCT DESIGN`), bold high-contrast headline, and high-tech mint-accent call-to-action button (`#7ef3c4`).
- **Interactive Projects Hub**: Filter projects dynamically by discipline (`CAD / Mechanical Design`, `FEA & Simulation`, `Robotics & Mechatronics`, `Thermal & Fluids`) and open in-depth engineering breakdown modals.
- **Publications Library**: Formatted journal & conference papers with abstract toggle accordions and one-click **BibTeX citation copying**.
- **Experience Timeline**: Vertical career progression timeline connecting industry internships, research assistantships, and collegiate design team leadership.
- **Academic Qualifications & Skills Matrix**: Verified degree credentials, GPA, ASME/SolidWorks professional certifications, and interactive skill bars.
- **Honors & Testimonials**: Competition awards and recommendations/comments from mentors and professors with a comment submission form.

## 🚀 How to Open & Use (Zero Coding Needed)

1. Navigate to `C:\Users\Oshan\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\mechanical-portfolio` in your Windows File Explorer.
2. Double-click `index.html` to launch it immediately in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any modern web browser.
3. To customize your information, open any `.html` file in **Notepad** or VS Code, change the text, and press `Ctrl + S`.
4. To add your own CAD renders or photos, save them to the `images/` folder and update the image file name.
