STEP 2: HOW TO CHANGE YOUR NAME, BIO & TEXT (SUPER EASY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every file is written in plain English text. You do NOT need any special coding
software—you can use "Notepad" that comes free with Windows!

1. Right-click on "index.html" (or any page you want to edit).
2. Click "Open with" -> choose "Notepad" (or VS Code if you have it).
3. Look for the clearly marked labels:
   For example, search for: OSHAN
   You can change "OSHAN" to your full name!
   You can change "oshan.engineering@gmail.com" to your real email address!
4. Press Ctrl + S to save the file.
5. Go back to your browser and press refresh (F5). Your new text will appear
   instantly!
